Who We Are

UTAR E-Sports Club is dedicated to fostering a supportive community for both casual and competitive gamers. Founded with the mission to promote esports as a recognized collegiate sport, we bridge the gap between passion and professionalism.

Whether you are looking to climb the ranks in regional tournaments or simply find a squad to play with after classes, we offer the platform, coaching, and equipment to scale your talent.
