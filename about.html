About Our Club

Building the future of collegiate esports at UTAR.

Our Vision

To become the leading university esports community in Malaysia, recognized for cultivating top-tier competitive talent and promoting a responsible, positive gaming culture among students.

Our Mission

To provide students with professional training resources, industry networking opportunities, and a structured competitive environment while balancing academic excellence and gaming passion.
